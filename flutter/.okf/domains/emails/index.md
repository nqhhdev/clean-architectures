# Concepts
* [Email Errors](email-errors.md) - The errors of the Emails slice: InvalidId and NotFound; transport errors are CommonErrors.
* [Email](email.md) - An immutable freezed email with id, subject, sender, preview, receivedAt (UTC) and isRead.
* [Emails](emails.md) - The example slice: a read-and-triage inbox that lists, marks read/unread and deletes emails.
